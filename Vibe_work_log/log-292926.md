## Changes Made

### 1. Color Coding Standardization
Following your

 specification, all EMS measurement bars now use standardized colors:

| Measurement | Color | Hex |
|-------------|-------|-----|
| Main voltage | Dark blue | `#0000ff` |
| Other voltage (auxiliary, battery voltage) | Light blue | `#55aaff` |
| Current | Magenta | `#ff00ff` |
| Power | Orange | `#ff9900` |
| Energy | Violet | `#9900ff` |
| State of Charge | Dark yellow | `#ffff00` |

**Green and Red** are now reserved **only for indicators** (status lights), not for measurement values.

### 2. Spacing Fix for Multi-line Titles
- Changed fixed `height` to `min-height` for `.bar-label` and `.ems-bar-label` CSS classes
- Increased `margin-bottom` from `4px` to `8px` to provide extra spacing when titles wrap to 2 lines
